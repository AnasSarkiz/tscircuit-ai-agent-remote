We are replacing the old ER-TFT023-1 display because it is too small, does not cover enough of the enclosure/front face, and its portrait/FPC arrangement is wrong for the product.
New display requirement
Use a BuyDisplay ~2.8-inch landscape LCD, preferably ER-TFT028A3-4 or the closest BuyDisplay variant that satisfies the requirements below.
Before changing the PCB, download and verify the exact manufacturer mechanical drawing/datasheet. Do not assume dimensions, FPC position, pin numbering, connector pitch, or contact orientation.
Target mechanical arrangement:
- PCB: approximately 50 × 65 mm
- Enclosure/front face: approximately 60 × 75 mm
- LCD must be mounted LANDSCAPE
- Target LCD body approximately 69 × 50 mm
- Target active area approximately 58 × 43 mm
- It is acceptable and expected for the LCD to slightly overhang the PCB, because it is supported by the enclosure/front assembly.
- It must remain fully inside the enclosure.
- The screen should use substantially more of the front face than the old ER-TFT023-1.
Critical FPC orientation
The LCD flex/FPC must exit toward the LEFT or RIGHT side of the finished device.
Do not orient the flex toward the top or bottom.
Rotate/orient the actual LCD assembly as required to achieve this while keeping the displayed UI landscape.
Move J7
The current J7 LCD connector position was designed around the old portrait LCD and is no longer valid.
Move J7 to the appropriate left/right PCB edge to match the exact FPC tail position of the new LCD.
Determine J7's final coordinates and rotation from the manufacturer's mechanical drawing rather than guessing.
Requirements for J7:
- Place it close to the corresponding PCB edge.
- FPC should enter the connector naturally.
- Avoid unnecessary 90°/180° flex folding.
- Respect the manufacturer's minimum flex bend radius.
- Do not route the flex across tall components.
- Leave room to open/close the ZIF latch during assembly.
- Check whether contacts are top-contact or bottom-contact.
- Verify pin 1 orientation.
- Verify connector pitch and exact mating FPC thickness.
- Make sure the enclosure wall does not interfere with the connector or flex bend.
Placement changes
You are allowed to move components affected by the new display/J7 position.
In particular, review and move as necessary:
- J7
- U14
- C42
- C43
- any other component blocking the LCD FPC path or connector-access area
Do not move parts blindly. Preserve electrical and RF placement constraints.
The antenna/antenna keepout remains critical:
- Do not place the LCD connector, FPC, copper, ground fill, or moved components inside an RF antenna keepout unless the RF design explicitly permits it.
- Check whether the LCD/FPC itself overlaps the antenna region and account for its effect mechanically and electrically.
Electrical integration
Verify the exact LCD controller/interface from the selected BuyDisplay part.
Prefer a simple interface suitable for the existing MCU, ideally:
- SPI
- minimal signal count
- supported logic voltage
Verify every LCD pin against the manufacturer datasheet:
- power rails
- ground
- SPI/interface signals
- reset
- D/C
- chip select
- backlight
- touch interface if used
Do not reuse the old LCD pin mapping unless it matches the new panel exactly.
Update the schematic and PCB together if the pinout/connector changes.
Backlight
Check the new LCD's LED backlight specifications and confirm the existing backlight circuit is suitable.
Verify:
- LED forward voltage
- number/configuration of LEDs
- required current
- driver/current-limiting method
- maximum ratings
Modify the backlight circuit if needed instead of assuming the old LCD circuit is compatible.

Calculate:
- LCD coverage of the enclosure face
- LCD coverage of the PCB
- LCD overhang beyond each PCB edge
- enclosure margin around the LCD
The previous 69.54% PCB overlap result is not our design target. We want the screen to visually fill most of the front enclosure.
