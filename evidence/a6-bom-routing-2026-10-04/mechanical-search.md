# A6 primary-source battery and mechanical follow-up — 2026-10-04

Exact protected pack remains AKY2945/LP523450. Supplier specification: https://www.elektronik.ropla.eu/pdf/stock/aky/aky2945.pdf (release 2026-08-20). Page 5 inspected as battery-drawing.png: red BAT+, yellow NTC, black BAT−; yellow centre. No numbered outer contacts. Page 2 and product metadata contain an inconsistent "2 pins" field even though the drawing/description show PHR-3 and three wires. This cannot establish contact 1 polarity. Generic JST housing numbering and different LP523450 packs do not qualify this harness. J3.2 remains PACK_NTC; J3.1/3 remain open. No supplier messages were sent.

The same drawing specifies thickness expansion 7% ±3%. Therefore the previously allocated 10% upper expansion is manufacturer-backed; 5.7 mm maximum pack thickness becomes 6.27 mm. Dimensions are specified under a 300 gf measurement load; uncompressed installed shape, tape, lead exit and case retention still require qualification. Supplier product https://www.elektronik.ropla.eu/en/magazyn/magazyn/?ic=AKY2945 currently lists zero stock/MOQ 10; cannot claim immediate procurement. Search found AKY1178 with PCM/NTC/3C but same cell envelope and zero stock, no mechanical benefit. AKY2531 is an 850 mAh PCM/10kNTC/3C candidate with a different Molex connector and 5.7 mm cell thickness, not a qualified thinner substitute; no change made. No compatible thinner protected, 10kNTC, sufficient-current pack with exact confirmed harness was qualified in this search.

JST PH manufacturer's https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/ePH.pdf page 1 requires 0.8–1.6 mm PCB thickness. JLC https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities gives ±0.1 mm below 1 mm and ±10% at/above 1 mm. The existing nominal 0.8 mm trial can be 0.7 mm, outside JST's specified range. A nominal 1.0 mm board would be 0.9–1.1 mm and meet it, but changes the previous conservative height sum from 14.87 to 15.07 mm. This is a specific 0.07 mm conflict with the user's 15 mm hard maximum under the present flat-cover assumptions, not proof that all possible redesigned cases fail. Actual cover/adhesive/flatness/flex tolerances remain open. Board thickness and USB impedance were not changed without a validated mechanical/stackup replacement. Arbitrarily shaving walls/clearance is not accepted.

Display remains provisional bare BuyDisplay ER-TFT026-1 no touch, integrated ILI9341, 46×64×2.55 mm, 82.712% nominal physical overlap. Current official PDF https://www.buydisplay.com/download/manual/ER-TFT026-1_Datasheet.pdf exposes Rev3 text via web reader but drawing screenshot fails with cache miss and direct download returns HTTP403. Historical Rev1 drawing cannot establish current flex shape/thickness. Physical portrait fold, contact face, and which J7 contact meets display pin 1 remain unqualified; logical manufacturer pin-map tests do not prove mating. Existing reverse contact assignment is provisional and dependent J7 routing stays deferred. No 8051 kit or touch-only panel was substituted. Existing RF display gap 0.475 mm nominal /0.175 mm under stated dimensional/position allocation remains conditional, not full enclosure approval. J8 is removed; old A4 diagrams showing J8 are historical.

PCB remains 50×65 mm; external case maximum remains 60×75×15 mm. Speaker, motor, screw heads, wire paths/strain relief, PCB pin ends and actuator tolerances still require actual assembly-envelope review. No fabrication or physical fit approval.
